# Material Design Icons font

`materialdesignicons-webfont.ttf` and `md_icons.json` (the glyph-name to
unicode-codepoint mapping) are from the Material Design Icons project
(Pictogrammers), vendored here directly rather than imported from the
`kivymd` pip package at runtime -- see the comment at the top of
`app/ui/components/icons.py` for why.

The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
bundling and redistribution (including in closed-source applications) as
long as the font itself isn't sold on its own. Full license text:
https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-Webfont/blob/master/LICENSE

Project home: https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/
